Getting Online

Brazil's connection curve is one of the steepest in the world, but it starts later than the timeline suggests and it is measured in a way that changed twice along the way. This page shows the figures and the caveats together.

About this site

Every claim on this site carries the source it came from. Where sources disagree, or where a widely repeated figure could not be traced to anyone who measured it, the site says so rather than picking the more quotable number.

  • Comitê Gestor da Internet no Brasil (CGI.br) and CETIC.br
  • Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE)
  • Agência Nacional de Telecomunicações (Anatel)
  • Banco Central do Brasil
  • Planalto (Brazilian federal legislation)
