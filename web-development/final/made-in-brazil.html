Made in Brazil

Three cases where Brazil did not follow the internet's centre of gravity: a social network it adopted and no one else did, a law other countries went on to cite, and a payment system built by a central bank rather than a startup.

About this site

Every claim on this site carries the source it came from. Where sources disagree, or where a widely repeated figure could not be traced to anyone who measured it, the site says so rather than picking the more quotable number.
