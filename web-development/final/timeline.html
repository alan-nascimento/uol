Timeline

Twenty events between 1995 and 2026. The starting point is not arbitrary: May 1995 is when the Ministry of Communications opened the internet beyond the academic network, and the same month the committee that still governs it was created.

About this site

Every claim on this site carries the source it came from. Where sources disagree, or where a widely repeated figure could not be traced to anyone who measured it, the site says so rather than picking the more quotable number.
