Wireframes v1 - The Internet in Brazil, 1995-2026

Five pages, each shown at desktop and phone width. These are structural wireframes: greyscale, no real copy, no imagery. Grey bars stand in for text so that the layout, not the wording, is what gets judged.

If you are reviewing these, please do this

  1. Open a page on your own phone using the "phone" link under each frame. That is the only way to judge the mobile layout honestly.
  2. Answer the questionnaire. It takes about five minutes and there are eight questions.
  3. Tell me what is confusing or missing. I am looking for problems, not compliments - "it looks nice" is genuinely no use to me.
